wish nº 3

my session survived the train tunnel.

Granted ✦

Hoshi has Mosh, written from scratch in Swift. Switch from Wi-Fi to cellular, lock the phone, ride through a tunnel. When you come back, you're still there. If mosh-server can't start, Hoshi tells you why, shows you how to fix it, and falls back to SSH.

three sessions, two over mosh

wish nº 4

it dropped me straight back into tmux.

Granted ✦

Hoshi finds your tmux sessions when you connect, with their names, window counts and last activity. Pick one, or start a new one. It remembers your choice, so next time one tap gets you back where you were.

Hoshi's own git log, in tmux, in Hoshi

wish nº 5

I could check on my agents from the couch.

Granted ✦

Herdr is built in. Attach to a Herdr session, or jump straight to one agent's pane. See which agents are working, waiting or done, reply to one, and follow it on the Lock Screen. Hoshi even imports your server's Herdr keybindings.

real Codex, digging through Hoshi's Mosh code

wish nº 6

the keyboard knew what I was running.

Granted ✦

The key bar changes with the app on screen. vim gets :w and ^R. Claude Code gets ⇧Tab and @. less gets q, n and G. Hoshi reads the terminal title and switches sets on its own. Try it.

nvim MoshStateSync.swiftVim keys

wish nº 7

I could add the keys I actually use.

Granted ✦

Add your own keys, drag them where you want them, and build whole sets with their own match words and tmux-style prefix. If a command lives in your fingers, it can live on the bar.

wish nº 8

I had arrow keys without hunting for arrow keys.

Granted ✦

Long-press the arrow key and it turns into a joystick. Drag in any direction and hold to keep moving. You can also swipe along the key bar. Tap to place the cursor in vim, pan to scroll, and pinch to resize the text.

The small print, written big.

Is there a subscription?

No. $4.99 once gets you every feature on this page and every update after it.

Why charge at all if it's open source?

Apple charges $99 a year to publish on the App Store. The price covers that. Once it's covered, the price goes down.

Can I really build it myself?

Yes. Clone the repo, run ./scripts/bootstrap.sh, and open it in Xcode. You'll need a Mac and a free Apple ID to run it on your phone.

Does it collect anything?

No account, no analytics, no ads. Credentials stay in the iOS Keychain, and Hoshi only talks to your servers.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. Every session gets its own terminal in split view, and hardware keyboards work as you'd expect.

I have a wish you didn't list.

Open an issue on GitHub, or better, send the pull request. That's what wish nº 1 is for.
